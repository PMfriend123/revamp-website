About Brooklyn Tech

Brooklyn Technical High School opened in 1922 with a mission to prepare students for college and careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

In , Brooklyn Tech celebrates years of challenging students to explore their interests and expand their abilities.

During their first two years, students build foundations across several academic subjects. They then select one of Brooklyn Tech’s 18 majors, allowing their junior and senior courses to focus on a particular field.

From engineering and computer science to finance, media, law, and medicine, the major system helps students discover possible paths for their futures.
